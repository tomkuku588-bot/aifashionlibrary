AI服装库隐私政策

更新日期：2026-11-01 | 生效日期：2026-11-01 | 版本 1.1.0 | com.xmgod.aifashionlibrary

欢迎使用 AI服装库（AI Costume Gallery）。本政策适用于 AI服装库 HarmonyOS 版本在安装、使用等过程中对信息的处理方式。开发者/运营者为本应用的发布主体，具体以华为应用市场页面公示的实名认证主体为准；联系邮箱为 tomkuku588@gmail.com。本应用是一款完全离线运行的服装条目检索与提示词文本拼装工具，免费、无广告、无需注册，不联网、不申请任何系统权限。

1. 我们处理的信息范围及用途

本应用不收集、不存储、不上传任何个人信息。我们处理的范围仅限于你在设备本地主动产生、并在应用私有目录内保存的数据：

  • 应用设置：界面语言（中文 / English / 日本語）、主题（跟随系统 / 浅色 / 深色）、协议同意状态。
  • 衣橱收藏：你收藏的服装条目编号、你自己书写的备注、置顶标记。
  • 本机使用记录：最近浏览、最近复制与搜索记录，以及标签使用次数。
  • 工坊草稿：你在提示词工坊里当前选中的标签、参数与手动改写后的提示词文本。

处理上述信息的目的仅在于实现本地功能：服装条目的筛选与检索、提示词文本的本地拼装、衣橱收藏与备注、常用标签排序、「最近浏览 / 最近复制」展示，以及界面语言与主题偏好保持。这些信息不构成用户画像，不用于任何推送、营销或个性化投放。当前版本不提供账号注册或登录，不默认收集手机号、电子邮箱、精准位置信息、通讯录、短信、通话记录、相册内容、人脸、指纹等个人信息。

一句话结论：本应用读不到你设备之外的一切，也送不出去任何东西。衣橱备注由你自己书写，请避免在其中写入他人身份信息。

2. 信息存储地点、期限与删除

上述信息全部存储在你的设备本地、应用沙箱私有目录内的关系型数据库与首选项（preferences）文件中，未加密，不会上传至开发者服务器或任何外部服务，当前版本也不提供云同步或云端备份。信息保存期限取决于你的使用行为。删除方式包括：在应用内逐条取消收藏、清除搜索记录；在「我的」页撤回协议同意，同时清空本机使用记录与工坊草稿；在系统中清除应用数据或卸载应用；重置设备。

由于不存在服务端副本，上述操作完成后即无法找回，本应用也没有任何可以恢复数据的云端能力。若你另行使用应用内的导出功能把搭配卡或提示词文本保存到了相册、其他目录或第三方云盘，该副本的存储与处置由你自行决定并负责。

3. 权限与系统能力说明

本应用的 module.json5 未声明任何 requestPermissions，即不申请包括网络权限在内的任何系统权限；工程内也不存在任何网络 API 的引用。核心功能在完全离线的状态下即可完整运行，不以联网为前提。涉及系统能力的操作只有两项，且都只在你在界面上明确发起时发生：

  • 保存搭配卡到相册：使用 HarmonyOS 提供的 SaveButton 安全控件。只有你亲手点击该按钮的那一次，系统才授予写入相册目录的临时授权；本应用不申请相册权限，也不会读取相册中已有的任何内容。不使用该功能时，本应用与相册零交互。
  • 导出文本或搭配文件：使用 HarmonyOS 提供的 DocumentViewPicker 文件选择器，保存目录由你在系统选择器中亲自选定，写入范围不超出你选定的位置。

除实现上述功能所必需的系统能力外，本应用不以后台方式读取无关文件，不采集与服装条目检索、提示词文本拼装无关的个人信息。若后续版本新增权限或系统能力需求，我们会在相应功能触发前另行说明，并依法取得相应授权。

4. 信息共享、转让与公开披露

我们不会将你的个人信息出售、出租、交换或共享给任何第三方，也不会擅自公开披露。仅在以下情形下可能发生信息对外流转：你主动使用保存搭配卡、导出文件、系统分享等能力，把内容带出应用；法律法规、监管机关或司法机关要求；或者为保护你、其他用户或公众的人身财产安全所必需。在上述情形下，我们会在法律允许的范围内采取合理保护措施。

5. 第三方 SDK、对外服务与 AI 生成合成说明

除 HarmonyOS 系统基础能力外，本应用不接入广告、商业化统计分析、第三方登录、地图、支付、推送、崩溃上报等任何第三方 SDK，工程内的第三方依赖为空。本应用也不向开发者自建服务器或任何外部服务上传你的收藏、备注、搜索记录或设置数据。

本应用不提供 AI 生成合成服务。提示词工坊做的是检索与固定规则下的模板拼装：同样的标签选择必得同样的文本，运行时不调用任何本地或云端模型，不产出图片、音频或视频，也不做随机或概率性合成。因此《人工智能生成合成内容标识办法》所规制的「生成合成」情形不适用于本应用，你在应用内看到的文本不是人工智能生成合成内容。你使用这些文本在第三方绘画工具中得到的图片，其标识义务由该工具与你的使用行为决定，与本应用无关。

若后续版本引入网络服务、AI 服务或其他第三方服务，我们会同步更新本政策，更换版本号并在应用下次启动时重新征求你的同意，必要时通过弹窗、页面提示或系统授权流程取得明示同意。

6. 随包内容与第三方素材

本应用随包包含 600 条服装条目、126 个标签与中 / 英 / 日三语文案，均为开发者独立编写。随包的服装参考图（季节与派对子集）版权归原站点所有，其条款禁止再分发；这些图片仅用于服装展示，本应用不主张任何图片权利，也不以自有内容对其再授权；条目匹配不到图片或图片解码失败时，界面回退为代码绘制的矢量示意图。筛选与检索交互机制参考开源项目 Brilliant666/ai-costume-gallery-cn（代码以 MIT 许可发布、中文译文以 CC BY 4.0 共享），本应用在「关于」页与随包 notice.md 按上述条款署名致谢。

上述内容属于应用交付物与知识产权范畴，不涉及对你的个人信息处理。

7. 信息安全

本应用采用完全本地化处理与零权限设计，从架构上消除了数据外传路径；同时遵循最小必要原则，只写入实现功能所必需的内容。任何技术措施都无法保证绝对安全。

本应用的本地数据库与首选项文件未做额外加密：若他人物理接触你的设备或获取到应用数据副本，可能直接读取上述本地内容。本应用不提供设备锁或独立密码。你仍需妥善保管自己的设备、系统账号，以及你自行导出的搭配卡与文本文件。

8. 你的权利

你可以在应用内查看、更正、补充、删除衣橱收藏、备注、搜索记录与工坊草稿，也可以清空本地数据或卸载应用以删除全部信息；在「我的」页撤回协议同意会同步清除本机使用记录与草稿。由于当前版本不提供在线账号系统，访问、更正、删除、撤回同意等操作均可直接在本地完成，不需要向我们提出请求。如果你对本政策、数据处理或权限使用仍有疑问，可以通过本政策载明的联系邮箱与我们联系。

9. 未成年人保护

本应用内容为全年龄向，不含成人题材（年龄分级 3+），界面不出现任何暴力、色情或不当内容；随包参考图为无脸隐形模特或悬浮单品，矢量回退图形为平铺单品，均不含面部与裸露。本应用不含账号体系、不含社交功能、不含内购，也不向任何服务提交数据，因此适合在家长知情下由未成年人使用。

AI Costume Gallery Privacy Policy

Updated on 2026-11-01 | Effective on 2026-11-01 | Version 1.1.0 | com.xmgod.aifashionlibrary

Welcome to AI Costume Gallery (AI服装库). This policy applies to the HarmonyOS version of the app and explains how information is handled when you install and use it. The developer/operator is the entity publishing the app, as displayed on the AppGallery listing under the verified developer identity. The contact email is tomkuku588@gmail.com. The app is a fully offline garment-entry browser and prompt-text assembler: free, ad-free, no registration, no network connections, and no system permissions.

1. What Information We Process and Why

The app does not collect, store, or upload any personal information. What we handle is limited to data you generate locally on your device and that is kept inside the app's private directory:

  • App settings: interface language (中文 / English / 日本語), theme (system / light / dark), and consent state.
  • Closet favorites: the garment entry numbers you favorite, notes you write yourself, and pin flags.
  • On-device usage history: recently viewed entries, recent copies and search records, and tag usage counts.
  • Workshop drafts: the tags, parameters, and manually edited prompt text currently selected in the prompt workshop.

These are processed only to implement local features: filtering and searching garment entries, assembling prompt text locally, closet favorites and notes, ordering frequently used tags, showing "recently viewed / recently copied", and preserving language and theme preferences. This data is not used to build a user profile and is not used for push, marketing, or personalization. The current version provides no account registration or login and does not collect your phone number, email address, precise location, contacts, SMS, call logs, photo library contents, facial data, fingerprints, or similar personal information by default.

In one sentence: the app cannot reach anything beyond your device, and it cannot send anything out. Closet notes are written by you; please avoid recording other people's identifying information in them.

2. Storage Location, Retention, and Deletion

All of the above is stored on your device, inside the app sandbox's private directory, in a relational database and preferences files. It is not encrypted, is not uploaded to any developer server or external service, and the current version provides no cloud sync or cloud backup. Retention depends on your own actions. You can remove the data by unfavoriting entries or clearing search records in the app, by withdrawing consent on the "Mine" page (which also clears on-device usage history and workshop drafts), by clearing app data in system settings or uninstalling the app, or by resetting the device.

Because no server-side copy exists, such deletions are final and the app has no cloud capability to restore anything. If you use the export features to save look cards or prompt text to your album, another folder, or a third-party drive, that copy is controlled by you.

3. Permissions and System Capabilities

The app's module.json5 declares no requestPermissions at all, which means it requests no system permissions, including network access; no network API is referenced anywhere in the project. Core features run completely offline and do not depend on network access. Only two operations involve system capabilities, and both happen solely when you explicitly trigger them in the interface:

  • Saving a look card to your album: uses the HarmonyOS SaveButton security control. The system grants write access to the album directory only for that single tap when you press the button yourself. The app does not request album permissions and never reads existing album contents. If you do not use this feature, the app has zero interaction with your album.
  • Exporting text or look files: uses the HarmonyOS DocumentViewPicker, and the destination folder is chosen by you in the system picker; the write scope never exceeds the location you select.

Other than these capabilities, the current version does not read unrelated files in the background and does not collect personal information unrelated to garment browsing and prompt-text assembly. If a future version adds new permissions or system capabilities, we will explain them before the relevant feature is triggered and obtain authorization where required by law.

4. Sharing, Transfer, and Public Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, trade, or otherwise share your personal information with any third party, and we do not publicly disclose it without authorization. External transfer may occur only when you actively use features such as saving look cards, exporting files, or system sharing to move content out of the app; when required by laws, regulators, or courts; or when necessary to protect the personal or property safety of you, other users, or the public. In such cases, we will take reasonable protective measures within the scope permitted by law.

5. Third-Party SDKs, External Services, and AI-Generated Content

Apart from HarmonyOS base system capabilities, the app integrates no third-party SDKs for advertising, commercial analytics, social login, maps, payment, push notifications, or crash reporting; the project's third-party dependency list is empty. The app also uploads none of your favorites, notes, search records, or settings to any developer-operated server or external service.

The app does not provide any AI generation or synthesis service. The prompt workshop performs retrieval and template assembly under fixed rules: the same tag selection always yields the same text, no local or cloud model is called at runtime, no image, audio, or video is produced, and there is no random or probabilistic synthesis. Therefore the "generation and synthesis" case regulated by the Measures for the Labeling of AI-Generated Synthetic Content does not apply to this app, and the text you see in the app is not AI-generated synthetic content. Any image you obtain from a third-party drawing tool using that text is subject to that tool's and your own labeling obligations, not to this app.

If a future version introduces network services, AI services, or other third-party services, we will update this policy, change the version number, ask for your consent again on the next app launch, and, where necessary, obtain explicit consent through notices, dialogs, or system authorization flows.

6. Bundled Content and Third-Party Assets

The app ships 600 garment entries, 126 tags, and trilingual (Chinese / English / Japanese) texts written independently by the developer. The bundled garment reference photos (a seasonal and party subset) remain the property of the original site, whose terms prohibit redistribution; they are included only to display garments, the app claims no rights in them and does not sublicense them, and entries with no matching image or a decode failure fall back to code-drawn vector silhouettes. The filtering and search interaction model references the open-source project Brilliant666/ai-costume-gallery-cn (code under the MIT license, Chinese translation under CC BY 4.0); the app credits it on the "About" page and in the bundled notice.md.

This section concerns the app's deliverables and intellectual property; it does not involve any processing of your personal information.

7. Data Security

The app uses fully local processing and a zero-permission design, which removes the data-exfiltration path by architecture, and follows the minimum-necessary principle by writing only what the features require. No technical measure can guarantee absolute security.

The local database and preferences files are not additionally encrypted, and the app provides no device lock or separate passcode: anyone with physical access to your device, or with a copy of the app's data, could read the local contents described above. You remain responsible for the security of your device, system accounts, and any look cards or text files you export.

8. Your Rights

You can view, correct, supplement, or delete closet favorites, notes, search records, and workshop drafts in the app, clear local data, or uninstall the app to remove all information; withdrawing consent on the "Mine" page also clears on-device usage history and drafts. Because the current version has no online account system, rights of access, correction, deletion, and withdrawal can all be exercised locally without sending us a request. If you still have questions about this policy, data handling, or permission use, you may contact us through the email provided above.

9. Minors

The app's content is for all ages, contains no adult material (age rating 3+), and shows no violence, pornography, or inappropriate content. The bundled reference images are faceless invisible mannequins or floating garments, and the vector fallbacks are flat garments, with no faces and no nudity. The app has no account system, no social features, and no in-app purchases, and submits no data to any service, so it is suitable for minors to use with parental awareness.

If you are under the age of 14, you should use the app only after your parent or legal guardian has read and agreed to this policy. We do not knowingly conduct targeted marketing toward minors or intentionally collect personal information from minors that is unrelated to the app's functions.

10. Updates and Contact

If there is a material change to product features, processing purposes, data types, permission use, or applicable rules, we will update this policy, change the version number, and ask for your consent again the next time the app starts; continued use means you accept the updated version. You can read the full text of the version currently in force on the "Mine" page in the app, which matches this page. You may contact us at tomkuku588@gmail.com, or visit the GitHub repository at https://github.com/tomkuku588-bot/aifashionlibrary.
